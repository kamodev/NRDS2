Florida allows permitless carry. Why get a license?

Since July 2023, eligible adults can carry concealed in Florida without a license, but a Florida concealed weapon license is still valuable. It's recognized by many other states for travel, and license holders are exempt from Florida's waiting period on handgun purchases. Proper training also helps you understand exactly where and how you can carry lawfully.

Do I need my own firearm for class?

No. Rental firearms are available for the live-fire portion. If you have your own, bring it unloaded and cased, and we'll help you get comfortable with it.

How does the online course work?

You complete the classroom lessons online at your own pace, then schedule a short in-person session with an instructor to complete your live-fire qualification and receive your certificate.

What should I bring?

A valid government-issued photo ID, eye and ear protection (available to borrow or buy), and comfortable closed-toe shoes. We'll let you know if your class needs anything else.

I've never shot before. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Many of our students are first-timers. Instructors start with the basics and move at a pace that keeps everyone safe and confident.

Do you offer group, military or LEO rates?

Yes. Call the shop to set up a private group class or ask about current military, veteran and law enforcement pricing.
