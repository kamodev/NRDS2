FAQs

Contact Us
What does Colorado require for a new concealed handgun permit?

Under SB24-1174, starting July 1, 2025, new applicants must complete at least eight hours of in-person training. That training covers safe handling and storage, shooting fundamentals, state and federal law, and interactions with law enforcement. It must include a live-fire exercise and a written exam, and it must be taught by a sheriff-verified instructor. Our CHP course covers all of it.

I've never fired a gun. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Many of our students are first-time shooters. Courses like PISTOL-200 and our Women's Handgun series start with the fundamentals in a safe, supportive setting.

Do I need to bring my own firearm and ammunition?

Requirements vary by course. Each class listing on the schedule includes a "what to bring" section. If you don't own a firearm yet, contact us before you register.

Do you offer certification for First Aid and CPR?

Yes. Our Adult and Pediatric First Aid/CPR/AED course leads to certification. FA-100 is a shorter introduction for anyone who wants the basics.

Can I book private or group instruction?

Yes. We offer private lessons and custom sessions for families, churches, businesses and community groups. Use our contact form to tell us what you need.
