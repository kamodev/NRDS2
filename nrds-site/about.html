About Us

Everyday people with a passion for safety, preparedness and community.

Our story

Not just experts. Neighbors.

National Readiness & Defense is a group of everyday people who share a passion for safety, preparedness and community. We're experts in our fields, and we're also your neighbors, friends and fellow citizens. We know how important it is to be ready for whatever life throws our way.

Our team comes from all walks of life and shares one goal: helping others gain the knowledge and skills to protect themselves and their loved ones. We teach firearms, CPR and first aid, ham radio communication and disaster preparedness, because we believe everyone can and should be prepared for emergencies.

Our approach

We meet you where you are

Emergencies don't discriminate, and neither does our approach. Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned practitioner, we tailor each course to your level. You'll leave with real confidence and competence.
