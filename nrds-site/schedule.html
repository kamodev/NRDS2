September

National Preparedness Month

Small steps now make a big difference later. Get yourself, your family and your home ready for emergencies like:

  • Home fires
  • Power outages
  • Local floods
  • Wildfires
  • Flash flooding
  • Hurricanes and more

Start by learning the risks where you live. Then make a plan, build an emergency supply kit, and stay involved with your community's emergency plan. Remember those with unique needs, including medical conditions, disabilities, pets and neighbors who may need help.

Colorado

Concealed Handgun Permit

Colorado Senate Bill SB24-1174, signed in 2024, raises the training requirements for concealed carry permits. Starting July 1, 2025, applicants must complete a comprehensive course with at least eight hours of in-person instruction. The course covers safe handling and storage, shooting fundamentals, state and federal firearm laws, and proper interactions with law enforcement.

Applicants must pass a live-fire exercise and a written exam. Courses must be taught by instructors verified by a county sheriff. The law also bars issuing permits for five years to anyone convicted of certain misdemeanors.
