Colorado SB24-1174

What the new CHP training law requires

Starting July 1, 2025, new Colorado concealed handgun permit applicants must complete a comprehensive training course that includes:

  • At least 8 hours of in-person instruction
  • Safe handling and storage of firearms
  • Shooting fundamentals
  • State and federal firearm laws
  • Proper interactions with law enforcement
  • A live-fire exercise and a written exam
  • Instruction by a sheriff-verified instructor
